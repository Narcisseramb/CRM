Founder

One partner. Three languages. A whole island.

Rambelson Global is led by Narcisse Rambeloson, an economist and professional linguist based in Toamasina, Madagascar's largest port city. For nearly ten years he has helped organizations like the Peace Corps and Mercy Ships, international companies and private clients cross the language and logistics gap between Madagascar and the rest of the world.

Whether you need a contract translated, a company registered, a market prospected or a journey planned — you work with one accountable partner who understands both sides of the bridge.
